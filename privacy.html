Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 19, 2025

Introduction

PicCall ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

PicCall is designed with privacy in mind. We collect and store the following information:

  • Contact Information: Names and phone numbers that you manually enter into the app
  • Photos: Contact photos that you choose to add from your device's photo library
  • App Settings: Your app preferences and settings

Important: All data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your information on external servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Display your contacts as visual cards for easy access
  • Enable calling functionality through your device
  • Maintain your app preferences and settings

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, or any other purposes.

Data Storage and Security

All your contact information, including names, phone numbers, and photos, is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We use industry-standard encryption (AES-GCM) to protect your sensitive data.

Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it (for example, when making a phone call through your device's native calling functionality).

Permissions

PicCall requires the following permissions:

  • Photo Library Access: To allow you to select and add photos for your contacts
  • Phone Calling: To enable the calling functionality when you tap a contact

These permissions are used only for the stated purposes and are not used to access any other data on your device.

Third-Party Services

PicCall does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. We do not share your information with any third parties.

Data Deletion

You can delete any contact or all your data at any time directly within the app. When you delete the app, all associated data is removed from your device.

Children's Privacy

PicCall is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
