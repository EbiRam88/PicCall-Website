Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 19, 2025

🔒 Zero Data Collection

PicCall does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information. All data remains on your device and is never sent to any servers, analytics services, or third parties.

Introduction

PicCall ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how PicCall handles your information. The most important thing to know: we do not collect any data from you.

Data Storage

All information you enter into PicCall (contact names, phone numbers, and photos) is stored exclusively on your device. This data is encrypted using industry-standard encryption (AES-GCM) and remains on your device at all times.

We do not have access to your data. We do not collect it, we do not transmit it, and we do not store it on any servers.

No Data Collection

PicCall does not collect:

  • No analytics data
  • No usage statistics
  • No device information
  • No location data
  • No personal information
  • No data of any kind

The app operates entirely offline and does not connect to any external servers or services.

Permissions

PicCall requires the following permissions, which are used only for the stated purposes:

  • Photo Library Access: To allow you to select and add photos for your contacts. This permission is used only when you actively choose to add a photo.
  • Phone Calling: To enable the calling functionality when you tap a contact. This uses your device's native calling feature.

These permissions are not used to access any other data on your device, and we do not collect or transmit any information obtained through these permissions.

Third-Party Services

PicCall does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data collection services. There are no third-party SDKs, tracking libraries, or external connections in the app.

Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data. You can delete any contact or all your data at any time directly within the app. When you delete the app, all associated data is immediately removed from your device.

Children's Privacy

PicCall is not intended for children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
